# Brand Color Guidelines

This document serves as a reference for the platform's color scheme to ensure consistency across different modules.

## 1. Command Center
The Command Center consists of all pages located directly under `src/pages/`.
- **Primary Color:** `#0A2A0A` (Dark Green)
- **Accent Color:** `#FFEA00` (Bright Yellow)

## 2. Pay-As-You-Go (PAYG)
The PAYG module consists of all pages located under `src/pages/payg/`.
- **Primary Color:** `#008613` (Brand Green)
- **Accent Color:** `#FFEA00` (Bright Yellow)

## 3. Shared Accent
- **Yellow:** `#FFEA00` is used as the standard accent color for both modules.
